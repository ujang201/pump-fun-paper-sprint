Build a paper-trading bot for the pump.fun paper sprint bounty (main.ts, Node 22, agent-fun/replay/v2 over stdin/stdout).

The bot reads hello/batch/done NDJSON lines from the harness, tracks each mint's bonding-curve price from virtual reserves, and replies to every batch with buy/sell orders. Strategy: momentum scalper — buy fresh coins (first seen within ~10 minutes) only after the price is up at least 8% from its first print with buys dominating volume, fixed 0.2 SOL tickets capped at 0.8% of real reserves, max 5 open positions, one entry per mint ever. Exit on +30% take-profit, -25% stop-loss, 15% give-back from the peak once up 10%, or a ~16-minute hard time limit. Fully deterministic: no clock, no randomness, no network. Verify with the kit's grade-local (process driver), check determinism by running twice, and test in a read-only sandbox as nobody before submitting.
